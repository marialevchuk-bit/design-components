Handoff notes

    
    
      
  • Timing and easing match the design file: ob-pulse 2.4 s ease-out, ob-drop 620 ms, ob-up 320 ms ease, ob-pop 420 ms, ob-fill 380 ms, ob-sel at 2.1 s.
    • 
      
  • Colours are the light-theme values from the design (accent #003DF5, soft #E5EBFE, border #D5D8E0). Place the animations on a white screen.
    • 
      
  • Text in steps 2 and 3 (plan chips and step labels) is outlined Inter, so it renders the same in lottie-web, lottie-ios and lottie-android. To translate it, swap in a text layer or regenerate the file.
    • 
      
  • The benefit list under step 1 ("No roaming charges"…) is a plain CSS fade-up in the design and isn't part of the Lottie file.
