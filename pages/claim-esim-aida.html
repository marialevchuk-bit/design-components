    Quick answers

    Can I install before travel?

    Yes, and we recommend it. Install the eSIM at home over Wi‑Fi. It switches on when you arrive at your destination.

    Is my phone compatible?

    Most phones from 2019 onwards support eSIM. Your phone also needs to be unlocked. Check your phone.

    Will I keep my phone number?

    Yes. Your own SIM stays in your phone for calls and texts. The eSIM only handles your mobile data.

    Will my eSIM work as soon as I land?

    Yes. Make sure the eSIM is set as your mobile data line and data roaming is on for it. The install email shows you how.

    Can I buy more data later?

    Yes. Top up any time in the Hubby app, and your 20% AIDA discount still applies.
