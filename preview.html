01 Read this first

Five decisions shape how everything is structured. The rest is detail.

  • 01The brand colour is a variable, not a value. Orange #FF8800 is only Hubby's own default — every partner overrides it at runtime. Buttons, pills, badges, progress fills and the nav bar all bind to --brand. Never paint a brand hex.
  • 02There is no dark mode. One light theme. A dark variant would be new design work, not a port — don't build one unless product asks.
  • 03One button, three variants. 52px tall, 10px radius, 18/500 label. Filled, outlined, soft. No other button spec exists.
  • 04Flat, with one exception. Pages, cards, bars and buttons cast no shadow. Only dialogs and bottom sheets lift.
  • 05Touch only — no hover. Design pressed, focused and disabled. Hover is deliberately switched off in code.

02 Partner theming

Hubby ships white-labelled: the same screen renders in a partner's colour, fetched at runtime. In this system that's a single override — set the brand tokens on a wrapper class and the whole subtree re-skins. Try it:

Popular
Design constraint

Anything a partner can recolour must bind to --brand (or --brand-soft / --brand-pressed). Contrast limits between a partner colour and white labels are owned by product & partnerships at onboarding — not the library.
