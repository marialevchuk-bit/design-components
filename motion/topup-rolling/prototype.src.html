Interruption

Never blocks input. A tap anywhere works mid-roll. If a second top-up arrives mid-flight, jump to the end state, then roll from there. If the card leaves the screen, jump to the end.

Reduced motion

When MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf is true: no roll. The old card content cross-fades to the new one in 150 ms (AnimatedSwitcher), meter included.

Edge cases

Top-up under 1 GB (no whole step): no roll; decimals, total and meter swap in 300 ms. Already two digits (12 → 22): the tens reel rolls with the carry, no entrance. The roll is always 1300 ms, however many steps.
