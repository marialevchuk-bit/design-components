ANIMATION SPEC: eSIM call-to-action icons
Screen / component: eSIM call-to-action button (Activate / Set up / Connect)
Purpose: Draws the eye to the one action the traveller needs to take next,
  and hints at what it does (download, switch on, connect).
Trigger: Shown on screen. Each icon starts its loop after a short delay
  and repeats while the button is visible. Tapping the button works at any
  moment; the loop never blocks input.

All positions are in the icon's 24-unit grid (the icon renders at 20px).
Each icon plays once a minute and is still for the rest of the minute.
Time 0 = start of one loop.

1. DOWNLOAD ARROW (Activate your eSIM)
   Loop 60s (once a minute). First loop starts 1000ms after the button appears.
   The arrow (stem + head) is clipped to the area above the tray line
   (y 0.7 to 19.2); the tray line never moves.
   a. Drop out   0-380ms     translateY 0 to +22 (gone below the clip)
                             Curves.easeInCubic
   b. Gap        380-440ms   nothing visible but the tray
   c. Fall in    440-820ms   translateY -16 to +1 (slightly past rest)
                             Curves.easeOutCubic
   d. Settle     820-1060ms  translateY +1 to 0
                             Curves.easeInOut
   e. Still      1060ms-60s

2. TOGGLE (Set up your eSIM)
   Loop 60s (once a minute). First loop starts 1000ms after the button appears.
   Off: outlined track, white knob on the left (x 8).
   On:  white track, knob in the button colour #0D112E on the right (x 16).
   a. Switch on  0-400ms     knob x 8 to 16; track fill #0D112E to white;
                             knob white to #0D112E. Curves.fastOutSlowIn
   b. Hold on    400-1800ms
   c. Switch off 1800-2200ms the same move played backwards
   d. Still      2200ms-60s

3. LIGHTNING (Connect eSIM)
   Loop 60s (once a minute). First loop starts 1000ms after the button appears.
   The bolt is one stroke drawn from its top point:
   (13,2) > (3,14) > (12,14) > (11,22) > (21,10) > (12,10) > (13,2).
   a. Retract    0-400ms     the stroke disappears from its start forwards
                             (visible part = [p x length, length])
                             Curves.easeInOut
   b. Empty      400-750ms
   c. Draw       750-1300ms  the stroke draws from the top point
                             (visible part = [0, p x length])
                             Curves.easeOutCubic
   d. Charge     1400-2000ms scale 1 to 1.04 (300ms easeOut) and back
                             (300ms easeInOut), around the icon centre
                 2050-2650ms the same pulse again
   e. Still      2650ms-60s

Stagger / delays: per icon as above. When all three buttons are on one
  screen, they enter one after another, 170ms apart (fade in, 300ms), and
  each icon keeps its own loop.
Interruption: None needed; loops never block input. Pause the loops when
  the button scrolls off screen or the app goes to the background.
Reduced motion (MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf == true):
  No loops. Each icon shows its rest state: arrow in place, toggle off,
  full bolt.
Suggested Flutter approach: One repeating AnimationController per icon,
  started after its delay, driving a CustomPainter. The lightning uses
  PathMetric.extractPath for the draw and retract. Reference code:
  cta_icon_animations.dart (EsimCtaButton + the three icons).
Assets: none (painted in code, no Lottie needed).

Open question for design: the hubby-motion guidelines ask decorative loops
  to stop eventually. Once a minute is being tested; alternatives are a
  shorter loop (about every 5s) or 2 to 3 plays, then stop at rest.
