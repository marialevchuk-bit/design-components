eSIM call-to-action icons

Three looping icon animations for the eSIM buttons, rebuilt from the approved video and set in GeneralSans, with calmer timing and 20px icons. Each icon now plays every 30 seconds; switch the cadence below to compare with the shorter loop. Pick a button to review it on its own, or see all three together.

Interruption

The loops never block input: the button can be tapped at any moment. Pause the loop when the button is off screen or the app is in the background.

Reduced motion

When MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf is true, nothing loops. Each icon shows its rest state: arrow in place, toggle off, full bolt.

Open question

Every 30 seconds, being tested. Alternatives: the shorter loop (about every 5 s), or play 2 to 3 times and stop at rest.
